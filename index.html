In honor of the 21 Coptic Martyrs of Libya

The 21 Martyrs

The 21 were mostly Egyptian migrant workers—20 from Egypt and one Ghanaian man, Matthew Ayariga. They were kidnapped in Libya and murdered by ISIS on February 15, 2015, after refusing to renounce their Christian faith. The Coptic Orthodox Church subsequently recognized them as martyrs and saints.

They became known as the 21 Coptic Martyrs of Libya after a video of their execution was released by ISIS on February 15, 2015. The men were depicted wearing orange jumpsuits and were killed on a beach near Sirte. The video was deleberetely produced as a source of propaganda, but the men's deaths subsequently became a source of enormoes religious significance for the Coptic Orthodox Church and the wider Christian community.

The Coptic Orthodox Church officially recognized the 21 as martyrs and commemorates them on February 15. Their names are remebmered individually rather than treating them as an anonymous group.
